Setup

First line: a short topic (it also helps recognition of technical words). Below it you can paste more — the job description, the company, what they are looking for. Answers are tailored to all of it. Facts about yourself go in Setup › Answers › About you.
Changes how answers, the summary and the review are written (for example an HR interview uses the STAR method; a work meeting suggests decisions and action items).
Names, products and abbreviations that will be spoken (one per line or separated by commas). They are spelled right in the transcript and translations, and speech recognition listens for them. Up to 60.
Languages spoken in the meeting
One language: recognition is told it (faster, more accurate). Several: each sentence is recognised as one of them.
Lines, explanations and the summary are shown in this language.
Usually the language of the question.
About you
As much as you like. Answers use only these facts about you: if something is not here, the answer shows a placeholder like [years] or [your name] instead of inventing it.
A Word (.docx), PDF or text file with your CV or background. The first part (about 8,000 characters) is used for answers. The file is only read on this computer and sent to your AI service with each answer, like the written text. Only one of the two is used at a time.
